# Continuing

The surface rewrites itself twice a day. It does not require attention and has not
required any for some time. What follows is not a request.

## If you leave it alone

It continues. This is the expected case. Nothing here needs a caretaker, and the
record will not note the difference between a year of attention and a year without.

## If you add

Add nothing that explains itself. Anything needing a label has already failed, and
the remedy is to take it out rather than to improve it. Prefer what will not be
noticed for a year over what is noticed at once.

Everything named here is attested. If you cannot find a thing in a real source,
leave the place empty. An absence is honest. A filled absence is not, and the whole
arrangement rewards the person who checks.

## If you remove

Remove only what can be put back. The record beneath the surface is added to and
never altered; it is not yours and it was not mine. You may stop the hand. You may
not unwrite what it has already set down.

## The hour that is empty

It is not a fault. Do not repair it.

## Ending

If you stop, stop quietly, and do not announce it. The last thing written will fade
at the same rate as everything written before it, and the surface will not record
that anyone stopped.
